Honest limits

  • BridgeVoice only simplifies the wording. It does not and cannot check whether a notice is genuine.
  • Never share an OTP, password or PIN — no real bank or office ever asks for one. If a notice does, treat it as a scam, however simple it sounds.
  • This is not legal or medical advice. For a decision that matters, confirm with the office, bank or doctor directly — the phone numbers above are a good place to start.
  • The word list is written by hand, so a few hard words may remain. The meaning is never changed on purpose: unknown sentences are shown as they are.
